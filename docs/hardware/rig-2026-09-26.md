## LIDAR inverted (17:10)

The C1 now hangs upside down under the clamp arm. What that changes and what it doesn't:

- **The scan plane is still level.** Flipping a 2D LIDAR 180° keeps its spin axis vertical; the plane stays
  horizontal, an inch lower than before (window at the bottom of the unit now). Inversion does not aim it at
  the floor. Only pitch (tilting the axis) or a lower mount does that.
- **The mount is out of the plane.** Clamp and arm sit above the window instead of beside it, so the 360°
  scan is no longer occluded by its own bracket. Hung under the arm, the sensor can also sit a few
  centimetres off the floor in the trial position. Keep it inverted.
- **`/scan` is mirrored until the driver knows.** `rplidar_c1.yaml` has `inverted: false`
  (`src/prevera_bringup/config/rplidar_c1.yaml:6`) and the detector reads angles straight from the message,
  no TF (`fall_detector_node.py:162-165`). Upside down, +y is the sensor's right instead of its left. The
  fall detector is mirror-invariant, so 09-25 tuning still holds; LIDAR↔camera association is not. Set
  **`inverted: true`**, restart the driver, and verify in Foxglove: a person walking to the **camera's left**
  must appear at **+y** in the `laser` frame.

## Counter baseline scan (requested 17:15)

A short labelled recording from the counter position, LIDAR inverted and level at 115.5 cm, camera at
103 cm pitched ~15° down. The point of this bag: the LIDAR should track a walking person and **lose them
the moment they lie down**, while the camera keeps them the whole time. One recording, both streams, the
fusion argument in five minutes. It is also the "high, level" reference for the 2D-vs-3D decision.

**Preconditions:** `inverted: true` applied and the driver restarted (`guardian-down` / `guardian-up`)
with nobody in view; wait 10 s for the 30-scan warm-up. Camera tilted ~15° down. Everything below runs
from the OMEN terminal against `jetson`; all long jobs are detached (lesson 9); `ROS_DOMAIN_ID=42` matches
`guardian-up.sh`; the workspace is sourced before `ros2 bag record` (lesson 11).

1. Pre-flight: `/dev/video0` present, MJPEG 1280x720 at 30 fps offered, disk free on `/opt/nvme`.
   ```
   ssh jetson 'ls -l /dev/video* /dev/rplidar; v4l2-ctl -d /dev/video0 --list-formats-ext 2>&1 | grep -A3 MJPG | head -8; df -h /opt/nvme | tail -1'
   ```
2. Start the camera file (MJPEG passthrough, no encode load, wall-clock start time in a sidecar):
   ```
   ssh jetson 'D=/opt/nvme/bags/counter-baseline-1; mkdir -p $D; date +%s.%N > $D/camera.start; setsid nohup gst-launch-1.0 -e v4l2src device=/dev/video0 ! image/jpeg,width=1280,height=720,framerate=30/1 ! matroskamux ! filesink location=$D/camera.mkv > $D/camera.log 2>&1 < /dev/null & sleep 2; ls -la $D'
   ```
3. Start the bag:
   ```
   ssh jetson 'source /opt/ros/humble/setup.bash; source ~/prevera-guardian-lidar/install/setup.bash; export ROS_DOMAIN_ID=42; D=/opt/nvme/bags/counter-baseline-1; setsid nohup ros2 bag record -s mcap -o $D/lidar /scan /tracks /fall_events > $D/bag.log 2>&1 < /dev/null & sleep 4; tail -3 $D/bag.log'
   ```
4. Labels, in a second OMEN terminal (type a word, Enter, at each phase; Ctrl-C when done):
   ```
   ssh -t jetson 'while read -p "label> " l; do echo "$(date +%s.%N) $l" | tee -a /opt/nvme/bags/counter-baseline-1/labels.txt; done'
   ```
5. The trial (~4 min): `empty` 30 s with nobody in view → `walk-2m` cross the room at 2 m, `walk-3m` back
   at 3 m → `lie-2m` lie down sideways at 2 m, `still` for 15 s, `up` → `sit` on a chair at ~2.5 m for
   20 s, stand → `exit`, 20 s empty.
6. Stop, bag first so it flushes, then the camera (`-e` makes gst finish the file on SIGINT):
   ```
   ssh jetson 'pkill -INT -f "[r]os2 bag record"; sleep 2; pkill -INT -f "[g]st-launch-1.0"; sleep 3; ls -la /opt/nvme/bags/counter-baseline-1 /opt/nvme/bags/counter-baseline-1/lidar'
   ```
7. Write the result (how many scans, events by level, what the camera file looks like) in
   `docs/field-tests/2026-09-26-counter-baseline.md`. Expected: OBSERVE/WARN nothing during `lie-2m`
   (the plane is above a lying body), tracks during the walks, and a clean camera file with the fall in
   frame. If the LIDAR *does* see the lying body from 115 cm, that is a finding, not a bug; write it down.

Frame k of `camera.mkv` is at `camera.start + k/30` s; bag timestamps are ROS wall clock, so the two align

## The height problem

The URDF assumes a **0.65 m** level scan plane (`sentinel.urdf.xacro`), and the pre-trial planning notes already called that
too high to see a person lying flat. On 09-25 a lying person added zero points at the "original" height and
became visible only after lowering, but no height was written down for either run. Today's rig puts the scan
window at **1.18 m**. Level, that plane is chest height on a standing adult and never touches the floor.

**Resolved 2026-09-26 (Jeremy):** the LIDAR is level and the counter is where the rig parks between
sessions. **Trials run with the tripod on the floor**, the height class that worked on 09-25. The two
alternatives below stay for the record; option 2 is the one in force.

1. **The LIDAR is pitched down** on the flexible arm (option b in the pre-trial planning notes). Then the plane meets the
   floor along a line, and detection depends on where the person is:

   | Pitch down | Plane reaches the floor at | Slice height at 2 m |
   |---|---|---|
   | 0° | never | 118 cm |
   | 16° | 4.1 m | 61 cm |
   | 22° | 2.9 m | 37 cm |
   | 30° | 2.0 m | 3 cm |

   A pitched plane cuts a body at an angle, so the cluster footprint that `_looks_horizontal()` judges is no
   longer a horizontal slice, and the background model sees the floor as a curved wall of returns beyond the
   contact line. Workable, but it has to go into the URDF (pitch joint) and the detector has to be tested
   against it on the replay harness first.

2. **The counter is not the trial position.** The tripod goes on the floor for the clean-data protocol
   (≈ 0.3 m scan plane, the height class that worked on 09-25), and the counter is where the rig sits between
   sessions. Then the number to record is the floor-mount height, and the URDF gets that.

Either way: **measure the pitch** (a phone inclinometer app on the LIDAR's top cap does it) and **restart the
detector after the rig is placed** (handoff lesson 5). Do not record trials until the height and pitch in the
URDF match the rig.

## What this rig gives the fusion design

Both sensors move together, so the LIDAR→camera transform is a constant. Once the numbers above are in the
URDF, a LIDAR track `(x, y)` on the floor plane projects to an image row/column and a person box from RF-DETR
can be matched to the track by column overlap. That is the association step decision D0
([`docs/DECISIONS.md`](../DECISIONS.md)) needs, whichever side of the bridge fusion runs on. A pitched LIDAR makes the
projection a little more work (the track lies on a tilted plane), not less possible.
