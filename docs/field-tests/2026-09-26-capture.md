# Field test 2026-09-26 — counter baseline + floor trials (in progress)

## Calibration (21:50 and 22:03, sampler logs in `calibration/`)

Detector at the deployed config (`36ca257`, `min_range_m` 0.05), LIDAR inverted and level at 115.5 cm on
the counter, `inverted: true`. `/tracks` sampled with `~/guardian-tools/track_sampler.py` (largest track
with range > 0.4 m, 2 Hz).

**Run 1, `calibration-walk-045037.txt`:** walking the 2 m cross line back and forth for 40 s.
**Run 2, `calibration-stands-050329.txt`:** background restarted on an empty room, then stands at the
left end, right end and the X (8 s each), then out.

Findings:

1. **Camera-forward is −y in the `laser` frame.** Every person-like track sat at y ≈ −1.1 … −1.8 while
   on the 2 m line and at y ≈ −3.9 at the far end of the room.
2. **The LIDAR origin is ≈ 0.6 m in front of the tape origin** (the counter base). The 2 m line reads
   ≈ 1.3–1.5 m and the X (tape 2.5 m) ≈ 1.74 m; the centroid is the torso's front surface, so
   `LIDAR range ≈ tape distance − 0.6 m`.
3. **The ends of the 2 m line are invisible to the LIDAR.** Neither stand at x = ±1 produced a track.
   The scan histogram (`scan_probe.py`, 21:52) shows background surfaces at 1.7–2.1 m in exactly those
   bearings (−135…−120° and −60…−45°: the shiplap wall on one side, furniture on the other), and a
   person 1.85 m out in the same beam is inside `foreground_margin_m` of the background range, so the
   detector never marks them foreground. **Room constraint, not a bug: keep trials within ±0.6 m of the
   centre line.** The first walk's "one half missing" was the same effect plus the background having
   absorbed Jeremy at the end where he stood before the walk.
4. **Handedness:** from the room geometry (shiplap wall at −x is on the camera's right), camera-left is
   +x, i.e. the standard right-handed frame with forward = −y. `inverted: true` is correct. Not yet
   confirmed by a track at a known side, because of (3); confirm with a stand at x = ±0.5 later.
5. **Track fragmentation:** one person standing still at the centre produced a new track id every 2–5 s
   (ids 8, 9, 10, 11, 14, 17, 18, 22, 24, 28 in run 2) with extents jumping 0.2–1.4 m. Same mechanism
   family as the 09-25 re-spawns; the replay harness will get these logs as a second test set once the
   bags exist.
6. Blind scan sectors (no return at all): −45…−15° and +15…+30°, consistent with glass; near-sensor
   clutter 0.18–0.34 m across +105…+180° (the arm, strap and cables behind the sensor).

## Recording A — `counter-baseline-1` (22:11:38 → 22:13:54, 136 s)

Bag on the Jetson `/opt/nvme/bags/counter-baseline-1/bag/bag_0.mcap` (1.48 GB): `/scan` 1336, `/tracks` 1351,

`metadata.yaml`, the per-second `timeline.txt` (`bag_timeline.py`) and frames at the moments below are in
`counter-baseline-1/`. **No phase labels were typed** (`labels.txt` holds one pre-recording test line), so the
phases below are reconstructed from the camera frames against the LIDAR timeline.

| t (s) | Camera shows | LIDAR (`/tracks`, person-like) | Events |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–29 | empty room | nothing (clutter track only) | 0 |
| 30–33 | walking across the 2 m line, camera-left half | x −0.2…−0.8, y −1.4, r 1.4–1.7 | 19 OBSERVE |
| 34–43 | camera-right half / lane edge | nothing | 0 |
| 44–48 | back on the camera-left half | x −0.1…−1.1, r 1.5–2.0 | 8 OBSERVE |
| 49–59 | walking to the X along the right lane edge | nothing | 0 |
| 60–89 | **lying flat on his back across the 2 m line** (top and floor cams) | **still track at (−0.91, −1.57), r 1.81 m, head side, for 25 s** | 0 |
| 91–120 | sitting still in the office chair at the far desk | (−0.7, −2.6), r 2.7, is_still most samples | 133 OBSERVE, **1 WARN at 115 s** |
| 121–134 | empty | nothing | 0 |

Findings:

1. **Handedness, empirical:** camera-left ↔ **negative x**, straight ahead ↔ **−y**. Resolved at 22:21:
   the C1 was **on its side (89.9° on the gauge), scan plane vertical along the centre line**, so the
   apparent mirroring was the sensor's rotation, not the driver. `inverted` is back to `false`.
2. **The LIDAR held a still track on the lying person for 25 s** (rows 60–89) because its plane was
   **vertical along the centre line** (see 1): a body lying on that line is inside the plane. The same
   geometry explains 4: anything off the centre line is out of the plane. Recording A is therefore a
   "vertical LIDAR" dataset, not the counter-height horizontal baseline the runbook intended; the baseline
   is re-recorded as `counter-baseline-2` after the remount.
3. **Sitting still in a chair produced a WARN** (sustained-stillness path, 4 s) after 30 s of OBSERVE.
   Under the vertical plane (see 1) a seated person is cut lengthwise into a long, still cluster, so this is
   an artifact of that orientation, not a counter-height false alarm; Recording B (horizontal) had none.
4. The camera-right half of the 2 m line and the right lane edge never produced a track (rows 34–43,
   49–59): the same background-margin effect as in the calibration, on the +x side.

Findings:

1. **The horizontal 121 cm plane loses the person the moment they go below it and never sees them again
   until they stand**: 39 s (103–141) with no track and no event while both cameras hold him the whole time.
   This is the baseline number for the 2D-vs-3D decision and the fusion write-up: LIDAR-only recall for a
   floor-level person at this mount height is 0.
2. **Zero false alarms** in 165 s (no OBSERVE, no WARN), including the desk stand and the walks.
3. **Frame confirmed by the desk stand**: the far desk is camera-left in the C920 frame and reads +x, so
   camera-left = +x, forward = +y (right-handed).
4. The whole 2 m line is tracked without gaps at this mount (contrast Recording A).
