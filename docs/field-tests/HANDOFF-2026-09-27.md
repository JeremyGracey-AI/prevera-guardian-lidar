## Lessons learned (tonight)

**Rig**
1. **A flexible-arm mount can rotate the LIDAR 90° during a head rework and nobody notices.** Recording A was
   made with the scan plane vertical. Gauge the C1's top in two directions after every touch, and run
   `scan_probe.py` after every restart: a horizontal plane shows walls at real ranges all round and nothing at
   floor range; a vertical one shows the floor at 1 m and the ceiling behind.
2. **`inverted:` on sllidar reverses the angle order; it does not describe orientation.** The "mirrored frame"
   was the sensor's rotation. Handedness is settled empirically (a stand at a known side), not from the flag.
3. A level 2D plane at **121 cm sees nothing of a person on the floor** (recall 0 over 39 s) and raises **zero
   false alarms** over 165 s. A vertical plane along a lane sees a lying person in that lane only. The floor
   mount is the only 2D configuration left that could see a person anywhere on the floor; the 3D question stays open until it is tried.
4. The LIDAR origin sits ≈ 0.6 m in front of the counter base: `LIDAR range ≈ tape distance − 0.6 m`.
5. Room: the near walls behind the rig are at 1.6–2.1 m on every bearing; two glass sectors (−45…−15°,
   +15…+30°) return nothing; the far room is 4–10 m. Whole 2 m line is visible from the upright mount.

**Cameras**
6. `usb_cam` 0.8.1 with `pixel_format:=raw_mjpeg` publishes noise; `mjpeg2rgb` works (30 Hz, ~60 % of one core
   per camera). The node segfaults on SIGINT (apport fires); stop it with SIGTERM (`guardian-cams-down.sh`).
7. Two 720p30 MJPEG streams write ~11 MB/s into the bag (1.5–1.8 GB per 2.5 min). Fine on the NVMe; copy
   with rsync, not scp, and one source per call.

**Process**
8. **Labels failed twice.** Both bags were labelled after the fact from camera frames (`bag_frames.py` +
   `bag_timeline.py`, ~5 min per bag). Tomorrow: open the label window, type a test line, `cat` the file, and
   only then start the bag. The label file must show a line stamped *after* the bag's start time.
9. **Chat cues are too slow to sync on** (10–30 s). Start a detached sampler/recorder with a wide window
   before asking for the action, and read the file afterwards.
10. `pkill -f <pattern>` still must never share a command line with the launch it targets (lesson 8 of 09-26
    bit again at 17:58; `guardian-cams-down.sh` exists for that reason).
11. The Mac Studio reaches the Jetson directly (same router, `ssh <user>@<jetson-ip>`); the "no route"
    at 16:40 was an ARP miss cleared by a ping sweep. Jetson-side commands run from the Mac session; Jeremy does
    the physical steps and the `sudo` lines.
12. Background absorption in walking form: standing at one end of a line for >2 min before a walk baked that
    spot into the median; the walk then flickered in and out (calibration 1). Restart the detector on an empty
    room before every recording; nobody stands still anywhere for more than a few seconds beforehand.
